A UK property company building a practical app for landlords

Urban Wholesalers Properties LTD is a property company registered in England and Wales. We're developing an Android app that keeps rental records organised: properties, rooms, tenants, rent and payments, all in one place.

The idea is simple. You should be able to see what has been paid, what is part paid and what is still owed, without digging through notebooks, spreadsheets and bank statements.

Everything a rent record needs

The app covers the records landlords keep every month, from who lives where to what has been paid.

  • Monthly payment status

    Each month is marked in green, yellow or red, always with a text label, so you can see who has paid at a glance.

    • Paid
    • Part paid
    • Unpaid

  • Properties and rooms

    Organise multiple properties and the rooms within them. Occupancy is worked out from tenancy dates.

  • Tenants and tenancies

    Keep each tenant linked to their tenancy and assigned room.

  • Rent and deposits

    Set a monthly rent for each year, and record deposit details separately from rent.

  • Simple date selection

    Choose move-in, move-out and deposit dates from a calendar.

  • Payment recording

    Record full and part rent payments as they arrive.

  • Payment history and receipts

    Review recorded payments and share receipts.

  • Rent reminders

    Optional reminders on your own phone when rent is due or overdue.

    Needs notification permission. Android can delay background tasks, so timing may vary.

  • Utility bills

    Add utility bills from the home screen. They're kept separate from rent and deposits.

The app keeps records. It doesn't move money. It doesn't email or text tenants, take bank transfers or collect rent online. Reminders appear on the landlord's own phone.
